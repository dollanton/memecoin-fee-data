The number the fee tables hide

A headline fee is charged per leg, and priority fees plus slippage come on top. Worked example, 1 SOL round trip at ~$150 SOL: 1% platform fee (0.02 SOL) plus priority (0.001 to 0.010 SOL) plus 0.5% to 1% slippage (0.010 to 0.020 SOL) totals 0.031 to 0.050 SOL, or 3.1% to 5% of the trade. The advertised 1% is not the real cost.
