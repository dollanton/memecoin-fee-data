# Memecoin Trading Platform Fees 2026: Structured Dataset

Standard fees, referral tiers, cashback and chain coverage for the five memecoin trading platforms traders actually use: GMGN, Axiom, FOMO, Trojan, GoodCrypto.

Snapshot: **September 2026**. Verified against official platform documentation.

## Files

- `fee-data.json` — structured fee data per platform
- `fee-data.csv` — flat CSV version
- `index.html` — rendered dataset page (GitHub Pages)

## The number the fee tables hide

A headline fee is charged per leg, and priority fees plus slippage come on top. Worked example, 1 SOL round trip at ~$150 SOL: 1% platform fee (0.02 SOL) plus priority (0.001 to 0.010 SOL) plus 0.5% to 1% slippage (0.010 to 0.020 SOL) totals **0.031 to 0.050 SOL, or 3.1% to 5%** of the trade. The advertised 1% is not the real cost.
